## R3-Maj-5: Figure 6 Quantitative Validation

**Reviewer comment:**
> Looking at the data in Fig 6, this does not look like a proper validation. There are plenty of blue (control) dots outside the gray band. And plenty of red dots inside the gray band. If this is to be taken as validation it is very qualitative. Maybe providing distributions from MRI studies?

**Response:**
We thank the reviewer for this important feedback. We have revised Figure 6 to include quantitative annotations showing the percentage of cases falling within the UKBB reference ranges for each panel. These percentages demonstrate that:

- **LV in controls:** 77% (males) and 69% (females) fall within normal ranges, validating anatomical representativeness for healthy hearts.
- **LV in HF:** Only 17% (males) and 33% (females) remain within normal ranges, consistent with pathological ventricular dilation.
- **RV in controls:** 100% (males) and 77% (females) within range.
- **RV in HF:** 75-92% within range, reflecting that RV enlargement is less prominent than LV dilation in heart failure, as discussed in the manuscript.

These quantitative metrics clarify that our control cohort accurately represents healthy anatomy, while HF cases show expected pathological remodeling patterns. We have also clarified in the Discussion (Section 5.3) that this comparison serves as an anatomical fidelity check rather than functional validation.

**Changes made:**
- Revised Figure 6: Added percentage annotations to all four panels showing proportion of cases within UKBB reference ranges.
- Clarified interpretation in Discussion Section 5.3 (lines XXX).
